ABOUT US


Welcome to Techzone Leicester, your trusted local hub for fast, reliable, and affordable tech solutions.
Based right here in Leicester, we specialise in keeping your devices running smoothly—
whether you need a quick screen repair, battery replacement, custom PC upgrade, or high-quality
mobile accessories.


Why Choose Us?

  • Experienced Technicians: Expert hands handling everything from cracked iPhone screens to liquid-damaged laptops and custom builds.
  • Fast Turnaround: Most smartphone and tablet repairs are completed on the same day so you aren't left disconnected.
  • Quality Parts & Guarantees: We use high-grade components backed by warranties on our work to give you peace of mind.
  • Local & Friendly: Proudly serving the Leicester community with competitive local pricing and walk-in friendly support.

Whether you're dropping off a faulty device or looking for your next tech upgrade, pop into our Leicester branch or reach out to us online
—we’re always happy to help!
